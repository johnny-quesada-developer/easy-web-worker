## The code

A returned value is always copied. To transfer a result, the method completes the message itself and passes the
transfer list to `message.resolve`. `onMessage<TPayload, TResult>().handle(...)` is the form for that.

<CodeBlock code={worker} lang="ts" title="buffer.worker.ts" />

On the main thread the transfer list is the second argument of the call. It is the only difference between the
two buttons.

<CodeBlock code={demo} lang="tsx" title="TransferDemo.tsx" />

<Callout type="warning" title="A transferred buffer is gone for the sender">
  After a transfer, `buffer.byteLength` is `0` on the side that sent it. Read what you need before the call, or
  use the buffer that comes back.
</Callout>
